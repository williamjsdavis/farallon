# References

Two papers used by this project were previously bundled here as PDFs. They are now cited rather than redistributed. Obtain them from the publisher through the links below.

## Fiore Allwardt et al. (2007)

Fiore Allwardt, P., Bellahsen, N., and Pollard, D. D., 2007, Curvature and fracturing based on global positioning system data collected at Sheep Mountain anticline, Wyoming: *Geosphere*, v. 3, no. 6, p. 408–421.

- DOI: [10.1130/GES00088.1](https://doi.org/10.1130/GES00088.1)
- Link: <https://doi.org/10.1130/GES00088.1>
- Rights: © 2007 Geological Society of America, as printed on the article's first page.
- Use in this project: Figure 1 (printed page 409) is the source geological map for the Sheep Mountain demo. See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
- Formerly bundled as `papers/Fiore-et-al-Geosphere-2007.pdf`.
